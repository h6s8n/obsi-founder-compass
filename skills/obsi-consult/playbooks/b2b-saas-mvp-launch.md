# Market Playbook: B2B SaaS MVP Launch & PMF Validation

Distilled from verified operator benchmarks (`Lenny's Newsletter` + Kyle Poyar / Yuriy Timen, `2026-11-21`). Reuse for 90 days before repeating baseline B2B SaaS searches.

## Verified Conversion & Activation Benchmarks
- **User Activation Rate** (reaching core "Aha!" milestone):
  - Median SaaS: `30%–36%` | Good: `~40%` | Top quartile: `50%–60%`.
  - Activated users should retain `>= 2x` better than non-activated signups.
- **6-Month Free-to-Paid Conversion:**
  - Self-serve freemium: Good `3%–5%` | Great `6%–8%`.
  - Sales-assisted freemium: Good `5%–7%` | Great `10%–15%`.
  - Free trial (all types): Good `8%–12%` | Great `15%–25%`.
- **Product-Market Fit Thresholds:**
  - Cohort retention curve flattens above zero; `>= 40%` respond "very disappointed" on Sean Ellis PMF survey; early customers exhibit "pull" (asking to pay early).

## 6 Macro Business Pillars
1. **Hyper-Specific ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):** Narrow beyond "SMBs" to one sharp persona with an urgent, painful workflow (`Head of X at Stage Y company`).
2. **Design Partner Supply/Adoption (First 5–10 Champions):** Hand-recruit 5–10 early teams to shape workflow trust and guarantee real usage before scaling acquisition.
3. **Distribution & Acquisition Channels:** Test where that exact persona already hangs out (direct outreach, community demos, partner ecosystems) rather than waiting on cold generic SEO.
4. **Aha! Activation & Onboarding Conversion:** Compress time-to-first-value (`Aha!` moment) and align it with the milestone that predicts retention.
5. **Pricing, Trial Mechanics & Unit Economics:** Choose freemium vs sales-assist vs time-boxed trial intentionally and benchmark conversion against 6-month cohort standards.
6. **Feedback & Retention Loops:** Run fast 24h stall/churn interviews and monitor cohort flattening + pull signals before scaling spend.
